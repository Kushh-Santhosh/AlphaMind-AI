# AlphaMind AI v3 — Private Beta User Testing Guide

**Welcome Private Beta Testers!**  
Thank you for participating in the AlphaMind AI v3 SaaS Private Beta. This guide will walk you through the centerpiece features of the platform and show you how to report feedback and test system capabilities.

---

## 1. Quick-Start Guided Walkthrough

### Step 1: Open Mission Control Terminal
- Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/mission-control` (or your staging URL).
- Observe the **Live Header Bar** showing market status, subsystem health indicator, and real-time SSE stream events.

### Step 2: Explore the 5 Virtual AI Strategy Funds
- Review the **Live Fund Dashboard**:
  - Conservative Capital Preservation AI Fund
  - Balanced Multi-Asset Growth AI Fund
  - High-Growth Technology AI Fund
  - Aggressive Momentum Alpha AI Fund
  - Digital Asset & Crypto Intelligence AI Fund
- Click **Inspect** on any fund card to launch the **Decision Inspector Modal** detailing the probability distribution (Bull, Base, Bear), SHAP factor importances, supporting evidence, and citations.

### Step 3: Test Global Search & Command Palette
- Press `Cmd+K` (macOS) or `Ctrl+K` (Windows/Linux) anywhere in the app to bring up the **Global Search Palette**.
- Type ticker symbols (e.g. `AAPL`, `NVDA`, `SPY`) or keywords (e.g. `conservative`, `rebalance`).

### Step 4: Submit Feedback or Reset Demo Data
- Navigate to **Beta Settings** (`/settings` or click `v3.0.0-beta` in the sidebar).
- Use the **Feedback Form** to submit bug reports, feature requests, or general thoughts directly to the engineering team.
- Click **Reset Demo Account Datasets** if you wish to restore initial sample data.

## 2. Keyboard Shortcuts Reference

| Shortcut | Action | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| `Cmd + K` / `Ctrl + K` | Open Global Command Palette Search | App-wide |
| `Escape` | Close Search Palette or Decision Inspector Modal | Modals |
| `1` – `5` | Switch between Mission Control, Research, Risk, Forecast, Settings | Navigation |
